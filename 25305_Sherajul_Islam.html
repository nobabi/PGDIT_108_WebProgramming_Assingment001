Bangladesh Divisions Overview

Dhaka Division

Lalbagh Fort

Historic Mughal fort in Dhaka representing rich heritage.

Ahsan Manzil

Famous pink palace of Nawabs in Dhaka city.

Curzon Hall

Beautiful historic building of Dhaka University.

Suhrawardy Udyan

Historic park of independence speech.


Chattogram Division

Patenga Sea Beach

Popular sea beach near the port city.

Foy's Lake

Beautiful lake surrounded by hills.

Ethnological Museum

Shows culture of ethnic groups of Bangladesh.

Cox's Bazar

World’s longest natural sea beach.


Sylhet Division

Ratargul Swamp Forest

Only freshwater swamp forest in Bangladesh.

Hazrat Shah Jalal Mazar

Famous shrine visited by thousands.

Tea Gardens

Green tea estates with scenic beauty.

Jaflong

Famous tourist spot with river and hills.
